# Road Trip Madness

A 3D driving game for phones and tablets that builds its world from public map data.

## The game

Pick a start location and an end location. The game plots the fastest driving route between them using OpenStreetMap data, and that route becomes your road trip.

As you ride, you need to collect fuel and various kinds of ammunition, find places to repair your car, and fight off zombies, outlaws and alien cat invaders.

You are on rails. You can't veer off into the wilderness: the only way to leave the route is at specific spots, by turning left or right. Those turns take you into a town or a roadside location you can explore for gas, repairs and supplies, and then it's back on the road.

When something attacks, the game cuts to an action view: your car keeps rolling down the road while the threats close in, and you tap to target them and use other defensive moves to survive.

Target devices: mobile phones and tablets (touch first, portrait or landscape).

## Original project description

> A 3D driving game that uses publicly available map data to generate the map. The user will select a start location and end location. The game will then create the route based upon the fastest driving path. As the user rides, they will need to collect fuel and various ammunition, find spots to repair their cars, and fight various things like zombies, outlaws, and alien cat invaders. The target device is mobile phones and tablets. The user will not have the actual ability to detour from the path, except at specific locations by making a turn right or left. It is very much similar to Oregon Trail, where you can't just go off and veer off into the wilderness. We need to create some type of interface or something so that they can explore a city and get gas or explore a location and get supplies. The action scenes where they are fighting would be another view where they are being attacked. They would have a car traveling down a road with zombies or something like that, and they would be able to click and target and perform various other defense actions.
